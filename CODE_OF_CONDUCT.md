# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and creators with respect. Discuss evidence and technical claims on their merits. Do not harass people, disclose private information, or misrepresent someone’s work.

If you experience a problem, contact [the repository maintainer](https://github.com/cheercheung) through a contact method listed on their profile. Use GitHub’s reporting controls for abuse. Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation.
