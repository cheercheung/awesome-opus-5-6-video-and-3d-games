## Stage 1 — Metric 3D camera rig and near-plane-safe UV homography

2D code paintings fall apart when perspective lines are guessed by hand in screen pixels. Define all major planes and props in right-handed world meters (`+X` right, `+Y` forward, `+Z` up) through a single pinhole `Camera3D`:

- Place `eye` and `target` off-axis (for example, vanishing point around `55%` to `65%` width rather than dead-center `50%`) so one side carries visual mass while the opposite side opens breathing room.
- Paint planar architectural surfaces (walls, roofs, paving, timber decks) in rectangular UV arrays first and convert to premultiplied RGBA. Build each `3 x 3` UV-to-screen homography directly from the 3D plane basis (`K @ [R @ e1, R @ e2, R @ (p0 - eye)] @ H_uv_to_plane` rather than `cv2.getPerspectiveTransform` on projected 2D corners), mask warped pixels by exact ray-plane depth `z_cam > 0.05`, and blend exponential ray-distance fog (`1 - exp(-max(0, dist - fog_start) * fog_density)`).
- Clip 3D line segments against `z_cam >= 0.05` before projection so geometry crossing behind the lens never wraps across the frame.
- On stone or masonry walkways, replace uniform rectangular grids with jittered seed centers in physical meters, perturb lookup coordinates with smooth bicubic noise, and evaluate second-nearest minus nearest Voronoi distance (`d2 - d1`) over a vectorized `3 x 3` cell neighborhood to carve irregular mortar crevices.

## Stage 2 — Unified 3D light solver and Fresnel water reflections

Lock a single normalized 3D light travel vector `SUN_DIR` pointing from the sky toward the floor (`SUN_DIR[2] < 0`, or explicit point-light coordinates) before painting any surface:

- Trace floor and wall coordinates backward along `-SUN_DIR` (`t = z / -SUN_DIR[2]`, using 6 fixed height-refinement steps when roofs are pitched or stepped) onto overhead roofs, windows, or awnings so floor light patches, mullion shadows, volumetric sunbeams, and prop cast shadows share exact geometric alignment.
- On puddles, canals, or wet stone, combine `cv2.remap` capillary-rippled horizon reflections (`y_src = 2 * horizon_y - y`) with 3D plane-mirrored quads (`pts - 2 * dot(pts - p0, n) * n`). Weight reflectivity by `fresnel_min + (fresnel_max - fresnel_min) * (1 - dist_t) ** 1.4`, where `dist_t = clip((y - horizon_y) / max(1, h - horizon_y), 0, 1)` is normalized screen distance below the horizon (`0` at the horizon, `1` at the bottom of the frame).

## Stage 3 — Depth-interleaved 2x PyCairo splines and isotropic gouache dabs

- Split midground structure and organic forms across 3+ depth bands from back to front so columns, arches, furniture, and plants overlap naturally in 3D space.
- Render crisp structural linework and organic silhouettes onto `2x` supersampled `cairo.ImageSurface(FORMAT_ARGB32, w * 2, h * 2)` layers. Stroke Catmull-Rom splines with tapered end weights, subtle hand tremor, and perspective width decay (`clip(1 / (1 + z / (fade_dist * 0.45)), 0.25, 1.0)`), then Lanczos-downsample to `1x` and unpremultiply alpha cleanly.
- Parameterize repeated natural or architectural details (foliage fronds, branches, cables, ripples, roof tiles) via per-instance seeded cubic Bezier curves that vary length, droop, and silhouette nicks per seed rather than stamping translated copies of one polygon.
- Break up flat cel fills with multi-octave bicubic-upsampled zero-mean unit-variance Gaussian mottling (`fbm_gouache` at balanced square scales `(72, 68)`, `(26, 29)`, `(10.5, 9.5)`, `(4.5, 4.8)`), 80 to 100 randomly angled soft convex-polygon flat-brush dabs at `1/4` resolution, fine isotropic cold-press paper tooth, and warm bounce light inside deep shadows (`lum < 0.28`). Never apply high-aspect-ratio row or column noise (`3.5 x 85 px`) to painted fills, which creates synthetic striping at `1:1` zoom (use stretched noise octaves only for horizontal water ripples).

## Stage 4 — Foreground circular disc bokeh, 35mm optics, and faint tape patina

- Frame depth with a near-camera silhouette element along one edge or corner, convolved in premultiplied RGBA using a normalized circular aperture disc kernel (`make_disc_kernel(radius=7.8)` with a subtle spherical-aberration rim), followed by a separate `cv2.warpAffine` `~1 px` opposite red/blue lateral shift.
- Finish in float32 RGB with 35mm optics and restrained analog transfer steps:
  1. Multi-scale highlight halation (`sigma = 6.5, 24, 72 px` screen-blended above `lum > 0.72` plus explicit specular glints, using a warm cream or scene-matched tint).
  2. Radial corner chromatic aberration and gentle optical vignette.
  3. Linear shadow black lift paired with a `C1`-continuous `tanh` highlight shoulder above `knee = 0.80` (`ceiling = 0.965`).
  4. Luminance-weighted silver-halide grain (`env = clip(1.12 - 0.95 * lum ** 1.55, 0.12, 1.12)`) so shadows hold organic texture while bright skies stay clean.
  5. Faint analog transfer patina: horizontal YIQ chroma softening (`0.8` to `1.2 px`) with slight luma ring and `cv2.BORDER_REFLECT_101` padding so borders never streak, sub-pixel per-line horizontal tape jitter, and `<= 0.8%` scanline modulation.

## Stage 5 — Deterministic render and 1:1 macro crop audit loop

2. Export the full frame alongside three `1:1` (`600 x 450`) actual-pixel crops covering the primary light aperture, a reflective or textured midground surface, and foreground hero props.
